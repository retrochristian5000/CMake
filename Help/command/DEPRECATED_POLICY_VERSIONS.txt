.. versionchanged:: 3.27

  Compatibility with versions of CMake older than 3.5 is deprecated.
  Calls to :command:`cmake_minimum_required(VERSION)` or
  :command:`cmake_policy(VERSION)` that specify an older policy version
  level will produce a deprecation warning in CMake 3.27 and above.
